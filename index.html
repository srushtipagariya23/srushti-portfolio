Srushti Pagariya - Service Designer

I Make Services Make Sense

I am a human-centred service designer trained to listen carefully, surface what matters, and design services that genuinely work for people. My work bridges creative communication and service design.

Selected Work & Case Studies

  • Inforens: UX Strategy & Service Design internship project focused on making a student platform feel as trustworthy as the service behind it.
  • Scottish Widows x GSA: Service Design & Financial Wellbeing project designing income protection for people whose lives do not fit a fixed salary.
  • Sweet Lies & Bitter Truth: Inclusive Education project using editorial design to open up stories about empire, labour, and identity for 13-16 year olds.
  • Bored Directors: Masters research project designing for the phone-free time that remains when the screen is put away in schools.

Core Skills

Service Design (Journey Mapping, Service Blueprinting, Co-Design, Systems Thinking), Research & Insight (Audience Research, Qualitative Interviews, Synthesis), Strategy & Storytelling, Design & Visuals.
