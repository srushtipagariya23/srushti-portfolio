Srushti Pagariya - Service Designer

I am a service designer interested in how people actually experience the things built around them. My work is about making services feel clearer, more useful, and easier to move through.

My curated projects include UX Strategy for Inforens, Financial Wellbeing for Scottish Widows x GSA, Inclusive Education with Sweet Lies & Bitter Truth, and Research for Bored Directors.

Skills: Journey Mapping, Service Blueprinting, Audience Research, Insight Synthesis, Brand Strategy, Narrative Design, Visual Storytelling.
